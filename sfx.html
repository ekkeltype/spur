Spur sound bench

Dev only. Every sound in src/audio/sfx.ts, synthesized live with WebAudio. The first click unlocks audio. Continuous layers are driven every frame, as the game does; toggles and sliders set what they're fed. The ford's water and the lurch follow “Listener: cab”; splashes, whinnies and cattle use the Pan and Gain sliders.

Offline render

Renders every sound into an OfflineAudioContext and checks it. Also window.renderAll().

    Spectrograms

    40 Hz – 16 kHz on a log scale, −100 to 0 dBFS; a tick every half second.
